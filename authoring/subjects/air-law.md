# Air law and operating rules: opening lesson

Status: DRAFT. Provisional South African subject; current syllabus mapping and competent review pending.

## Objective

Separate sources, permissions and responsibilities.

## Lesson

A reliable compliance decision identifies the jurisdiction, the type of activity, the applicable requirements and the evidence that each requirement is met. Begin with a proposed operation described in ordinary language. Identify who will operate, what aircraft is involved, where and when it will occur, and what the intended work is.

Separate pilot-related requirements from aircraft-related and operator-related requirements. A credential held by one person cannot by itself establish the status of an aircraft or an organisation. Likewise, agreement from a landowner does not establish the applicable airspace position. Keep evidence for these dependencies separately.

A current-source register is essential. Record document title, edition or amendment, relevant section, jurisdiction and the date checked. A search result, an old screenshot or another pilot's experience may identify a research lead, but should not silently become authoritative evidence.

When information conflicts, stop the compliance conclusion and resolve the discrepancy through the current authority material and competent training or operational personnel. Record what is known and what remains unresolved. This lesson intentionally supplies no numerical operating limits and does not claim complete regulatory coverage.

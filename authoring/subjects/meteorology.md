# Meteorology: opening lesson

Status: DRAFT. Provisional South African subject; current syllabus mapping and competent review pending.

## Objective

Check the location and time represented by weather information.

## Lesson

A forecast predicts conditions for a stated place and period. An observation records conditions at a reporting place and time. The crew's site observations add local evidence. Keep these source types distinct and record their coverage rather than treating them as interchangeable statements about the whole mission.

Terrain and structures can affect local airflow. A regional record may not describe flow near a particular ridge or building. Identify the gap and relate it to the planned operating area. Do not turn a general forecast into a guaranteed description of conditions at every point.

Information has both an issue time and a valid period. A newly downloaded document can still be old, and a recently issued product may describe a different future period. Check the actual metadata. Relate the evidence to aircraft limitations and the operation's reviewed procedures.

A decision log should record source identity, time, place, uncertainty, review points and the response to changing conditions. This lesson teaches information handling and decision quality. It does not supply numeric weather minima or replace locally applicable operational procedures.

## Review record template

Record objective ID, lesson/question ID, document title, edition/date, exact section/page, jurisdiction, reviewer identity, review date, decision and change note. A checked URL alone is insufficient evidence of coverage. Do not use recalled confidential exam questions, commercial banks or third-party course text without appropriate permission.

# Commercial Drone Operations

Status: DRAFT. Instructor review required. No licence, accreditation or operational authorisation is awarded.

Category: Drone Business & Entrepreneurship

## Outcomes

- Convert a client request into a scoped work order
- Identify operational dependencies before quoting
- Define evidence-based acceptance and handover

## Module sequence

1. Discovery and scope boundaries
2. Permissions, crew and operational planning
3. Pricing, delivery risk and change control
4. Client handover and retention

## Assessment plan

Begin with the lesson 1 desk exercise. Later modules need original scenarios and topic checks. Finish with a reviewed capstone tied to the outcomes. Suggested internal rubric: evidence and accuracy 40%, method and reasoning 30%, limitations and professional judgement 20%, communication 10%. This is a draft learning rubric, not an official examination scheme.

## Current authored material

[Opening lesson](lesson-01.md). Remaining modules are planned, not authored.

## Prerequisites and delivery

Basic digital literacy; simulator or authorised sample data where specified. No unsupervised real aircraft exercise is required by this draft. Practical competency needs an appropriate training environment and instructor.

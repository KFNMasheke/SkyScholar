# Lesson 1: Sell a defined outcome with visible assumptions

Course: Commercial Drone Operations

Status: DRAFT. Original SkyScholar authoring; technical and instructional review pending.

Estimated study time: 20-30 minutes including the desk exercise (author estimate).

## Learning focus

Convert a client request into a scoped work order.

## Lesson

A client asks for a roof inspection tomorrow and requests a fixed price immediately. A useful commercial response starts with scope. Ask what decision the inspection must support, which structures are included, what defects matter, and whether a qualified specialist must interpret the imagery. Capturing images and certifying a roof condition are different responsibilities.

Separate the work into preparation, capture, analysis and delivery. Preparation includes confirming access, operational feasibility, crew availability and required permissions. Capture depends on appropriate equipment and conditions. Analysis includes image review and traceable observations. Delivery includes agreed files, a reporting format and a review window. Omitting these stages from a quote does not remove their cost.

List assumptions that can change the work: access restrictions, weather, incomplete site information, additional structures, repeat attendance or a new reporting requirement. State who can authorise a scope change. A low initial price with an undefined deliverable creates disputes even when the flying is technically successful.

Define acceptance in terms the client can check. Examples include a complete agreed asset list, an image index, explicit limitations and the stated report format. Avoid promising that no defects exist merely because none were visible. Keep observed evidence separate from interpretation and from work outside the operator's competence.

Finish with a handover that explains naming, retention, permitted sharing and follow-up. Use fictional client information in the exercise. This course does not replace jurisdiction-specific operational approval, professional certification or a reviewed commercial contract.

## Desk exercise

Draft a scope for a fictional three-building roof image survey, including exclusions, dependencies, acceptance criteria and a change request rule.

## Marking guide

A complete response separates capture from specialist diagnosis, covers four work stages and includes clear acceptance criteria without unsupported safety claims.

## Reflection

Name one assumption you would verify before applying this lesson to a real project. Identify the source or competent person who could resolve it.

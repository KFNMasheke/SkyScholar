# Lesson 1: Build a portfolio that another professional can verify

Course: Drone Pilot Career Development

Status: DRAFT. Original SkyScholar authoring; technical and instructional review pending.

Estimated study time: 20-30 minutes including the desk exercise (author estimate).

## Learning focus

Build a portfolio around verifiable deliverables.

## Lesson

A strong drone career portfolio shows how you solve a defined problem. A collection of attractive images may demonstrate visual taste, but it does not automatically demonstrate survey validation, inspection reasoning or safe operational planning. Select evidence that matches the role you want.

Use a simple case-study structure: the client question, your role, the method, the checks, the deliverable and the limitation. State which work you completed personally and which work belonged to a team or specialist. Use only material you are entitled to share. A fictional training project should be labelled as such rather than described as paid client delivery.

Keep credential language precise. Completing a short course, holding a pilot qualification, being current for a particular operation and demonstrating a software skill are different claims. Record the issuer, completion or expiry date where applicable, and verification route. Do not describe a planned qualification as completed.

A development plan starts with role evidence. Read a small set of target job descriptions and identify recurring requirements. For each requirement, record existing evidence, a gap and the smallest credible project or recognised training that could close it. Avoid collecting unrelated certificates without improving your ability to perform the target work.

For this lesson, create one honest case-study outline and one skill-gap plan. The goal is international credibility built on traceable work, clear writing and reliable professional behaviour, rather than an inflated list of tools.

## Desk exercise

Prepare a case-study outline for a fictional mapping training project and a three-step plan for a target junior GIS or drone-support role.

## Marking guide

Expect clear personal contribution, labelled training status, measurable checks and a specific evidence gap. Unsupported qualifications or commercial claims fail the exercise.

## Reflection

Name one assumption you would verify before applying this lesson to a real project. Identify the source or competent person who could resolve it.

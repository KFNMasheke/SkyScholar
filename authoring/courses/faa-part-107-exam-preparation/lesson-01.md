# Lesson 1: Build a source-controlled examination plan

Course: FAA Part 107 Exam Preparation

Status: DRAFT. Original SkyScholar authoring; technical and instructional review pending.

Estimated study time: 20-30 minutes including the desk exercise (author estimate).

## Learning focus

Locate official US study and certification references.

## Lesson

This course is a United States study pathway. Its rules and certificate terminology must remain separate from South African training. Before writing a learning plan, use the FAA's current certification guidance and official study-material links to identify the applicable pathway and knowledge standard.

Create a source register with document title, edition or identifier, the section used and the date checked. Use the current Airman Certification Standards to organise learning objectives. A study guide supports learning, but an older guide can require checking against newer rules and official notices. Do not assume that a PDF remains a complete statement of current requirements because it is hosted on an official website.

Turn an objective into three kinds of evidence: an explanation in your own words, a worked scenario, and an original practice question with reasons for every option. A learner who remembers an answer letter may fail when the scenario changes. Practice should reveal the reasoning that needs attention.

Keep a revision log with topic, attempted question, error type and next action. Distinguish a knowledge gap from a reading mistake or a calculation error. Use a timed mock only after the supporting concepts have been studied. A practice score is a learning indicator, not an examination pass or a remote pilot certificate.

For this lesson, produce the source register and objective map only. Detailed numeric operating rules and examination settings require current-source checking and competent review before publication. SkyScholar does not issue the FAA certificate or guarantee an examination result.

## Desk exercise

Build a source register using the official FAA study-material page and identify two objectives to map to the current ACS.

## Marking guide

Expect US-only scope, document identity, date checked, section references and a clear distinction between practice and certification.
